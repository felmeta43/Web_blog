Our Journey

A History of Growth & Achievement

2005

Founding of Alliance Hospital

Established with a founding team of 8 specialist doctors and a vision to bring world-class healthcare to Addis Ababa.

2008

Expansion of Surgical Suites

Added three new operating theatres, enabling complex cardiac, neuro, and orthopaedic surgeries for the first time.

2011

ISO 9001 Certification

Achieved international quality management certification, reinforcing our commitment to process excellence and patient safety.

2014

Neonatal ICU Opens

Our state-of-the-art NICU began operations, providing lifesaving care for premature and critically ill newborns.

2017

Cancer Centre Established

Launched a dedicated oncology centre with medical and radiation oncology capabilities, serving patients from across the region.

2019

JCI Accreditation

Became one of the first hospitals in Ethiopia to achieve Joint Commission International accreditation.

2022

Digital Health Transformation

Rolled out an integrated electronic medical records system and launched telemedicine services for remote patient consultations.

2025

40+ Specialists Milestone

Our medical team grew to more than 40 specialist doctors spanning 15+ departments, cementing our position as a full-service tertiary hospital.
