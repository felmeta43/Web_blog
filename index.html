About Alliance Hospital

A Legacy of Healing & Excellence

Founded with a bold vision, Alliance Hospital has grown into one of the region's most trusted medical institutions. Our team of more than 40 specialist physicians spans every major discipline, united by a single mission — to provide every patient with the highest standard of care.

We invest in cutting-edge technology, continuous staff training, and patient-centred processes so that when you walk through our doors, you receive treatment that is both scientifically advanced and deeply humane.

  • JCI-accredited facility & internationally trained staff
  • Fully equipped ICU, NICU & surgical suites
  • Multilingual patient support team
  • Transparent, affordable pricing with insurance support
